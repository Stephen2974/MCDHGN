D1 missing-value policy v1.
For each CNV_AMP:<cancer> and CNV_DEL:<cancer> column independently, replace only blank cells in cnv_features.tsv with the median of finite observed frequencies in that same column. Observed cells are copied byte-for-byte in numeric value and are never changed. No labels, folds, test data, or graph outputs are used to calculate medians. Blank cells represent unresolved source observation, source-row conflict, or unresolved identity; they are not treated as zero events. This produces a finite 12944 x 32 matrix. The original cnv_features.tsv, feature_observed.tsv, and missing_reasons.tsv remain preserved for audit. The resulting graph is an isolated D1 variant and does not overwrite D0 or P0.
